================================================================================
                     TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK TIPS
================================================================================

  Issue: No voice sound
  Solution: Check speaker volume, browser volume, system audio

  Issue: Microphone not working
  Solution: Check browser microphone permissions, allow when prompted

  Issue: Voice commands not recognized
  Solution: Speak clearly, normal volume, quiet environment

  Issue: Theme colors wrong
  Solution: Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), clear cache

  Issue: Charts not toggling
  Solution: Refresh page, clear cache, check console for errors
